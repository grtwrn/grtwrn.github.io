About

I'm a computer engineer (Brown '21) who worked in hardware architecture at NVIDIA. Now I build full-time: small, real devices and tools that help people notice what's going on around them and inside them, and act on it. I'm applying to the MIT Media Lab to do this as research.

Projects

StoveDetector

A small sensor that notices when a stove has been left on and unattended, and sounds an alert before it becomes a fire. Started as a personal safety fix (formerly "KitchenEye"); now a hardware startup heading into its first pilot homes. Custom carrier board, Wi-Fi alerts, and a pre-pilot FCC compliance process I'm running myself.

stovedetector.com

Status: founder, pre-pilot, 2026

The Louis: MIDI Trumpet

An affordable MIDI trumpet built to look, feel and sound like the real thing, so remote-learning students could practice without the cost or noise of an actual trumpet. Real valve springs and mouthpiece, a 3D-printed body, an I2S mic reading the mouthpiece buzz, valve sensors, and a Raspberry Pi + Teensy for signal processing, all for under $150 in parts.

Senior capstone at Brown (ENGN 1001), April 2021, with Riad Hallal, Nathan Plano and Albin Wells.

Status: completed, 2021

Planter Waterer

A 3D-printed conical spike that sits in the soil and slowly releases water, so a potted plant can go longer between waterings.

Status: built

Future You: Authenticity Detector

Extends the MIT Media Lab's "Future You" work: records your face during conversations with your future self and flags moments of discomfort or disengagement, to help separate what you actually feel from the answer you've rehearsed.

Status: in development
